Accepting payments — how it works

  1. Upgrade to the Payments tier above.
  2. Create your own free Stripe account at dashboard.stripe.com/register — Stripe verifies your identity/business directly with you; we never see or store that.
  3. Create a Payment Link at dashboard.stripe.com/payment-links/create.
  4. Paste the resulting stripe.com link, your legal entity name, and your donation terms into your profile, then submit for review.
  5. Whenever a supporter pays, log it under Manage supporters — that's what shows the running total and supporter list on your public page and in the catalog.

Stripe's own processing fees apply to your Stripe account, not ours — we never take a cut of donations. We only ever accept stripe.com-hosted payment links (checked automatically), and any listing with a donation ask is reviewed by a person before it goes live — this keeps the platform from becoming a vector for scam or phishing "donate" links.
