Security — for both sides

We didn't want to build something bad actors could use — against the founders who list here, or against the people who visit them. So both directions get checked, not just one.

Protecting visitors from bad links

  • Every founder-submitted link is checked against URLhaus, Google Safe Browsing, and VirusTotal before a listing can go live
  • A flagged link blocks auto-publish — a moderator has to consciously review and override it, not the other way around
  • Link safety can be re-checked any time after publishing, since a site's reputation isn't fixed forever
  • We never iframe a founder's site — always a plain, visible outbound link, so you always see exactly where you're going

Protecting founders' own sites

  • Optional, SSRF-hardened hygiene scan of your own site — HTTPS, TLS validity, security headers, cookie flags
  • Subscribers can opt in to a "known vulnerabilities" report: CVE lookups, email-spoofing (SPF/DMARC) checks, exposed-config checks
  • Donation links are locked to Stripe's own hosted checkout — no bank details are ever entered on this platform, and we never touch the money
  • Any listing that asks for support is reviewed by a person before it can accept anything

None of this is a penetration test or a security guarantee — it's a set of honest, disclosed, best-effort checks, always framed as "met criteria X as of date Y," never as "verified safe."

How it works

  1. A founder registers and fills in their dashboard — link, banner, description, funding goal, donation terms, Stripe payment link.
  2. They brief their AI pitch assistant once — the part that actually removes the language barrier and the "founder isn't online" problem.
  3. The listing is manually reviewed before anything goes public — every time, no exceptions.
  4. The published card appears in the catalog, filterable by category and stage.
  5. Visitors browse, chat, and verify — clicking through to the founder's own live site, asking the assistant questions, and optionally supporting the project directly.
