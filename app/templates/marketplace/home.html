How it works

  1. A founder registers and fills in their dashboard — link, banner, description, funding goal, donation terms, Stripe payment link.
  2. They brief their AI pitch assistant once — the part that actually removes the language barrier and the "founder isn't online" problem.
  3. The listing is manually reviewed before anything goes public — every time, no exceptions.
  4. The published card appears in the catalog, filterable by category and stage.
  5. Visitors browse, chat, and verify — clicking through to the founder's own live site, asking the assistant questions, and optionally supporting the project directly.
